# Flat White — Brand & Menu

## Story / tagline

**Come for the coffee, stay for the camaraderie.**

We're here to be a part of your daily ritual and foster a space where you can
unwind, savor your coffee, and cultivate meaningful connections.

## Visual identity (from the menu board and logo)

- Logo: hand-drawn fluffy dog face in leaf green, with "FLAT WHITE" arched above and a small heart.
- Background: warm cream, approx `#FBF9F1`
- Headings: deep forest green, approx `#1E4D2B`
- Logo green: approx `#6BA54A`
- Accent: golden mustard, approx `#E8A820` (used for "Iced Only" and tea sub-categories)
- Type: bold, rounded geometric sans for headings; clean sans for items; italic green for descriptions.
