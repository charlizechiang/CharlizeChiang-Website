Flat White is a neighborhood coffee shop on Lincoln Avenue in Cypress. We're here to be a part of your daily ritual, and to foster a space where you can unwind, savor your coffee, and cultivate meaningful connections.

Some mornings that's a quick drip on the way to work. Some afternoons it's a long catch-up over a Spanish Latte, a ceremonial matcha, or whatever's new on the seasonal board. Either way, we're glad you're here.
