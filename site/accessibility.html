accessibility statement

Flat White Coffee wants everyone to be able to use this website. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA.

What we've done

  • Pages work with a keyboard and screen readers, with visible focus outlines and a "skip to content" link.
  • Text and buttons meet color contrast guidelines.
  • Images have text descriptions, and videos can be paused.
  • The site respects your device's "reduce motion" setting.
  • The accessibility button (bottom left of every page) lets you enlarge text, turn on high contrast, highlight links, switch to a more readable font, add text spacing, stop animations, and use a standard mouse cursor.

Ordering online

Online orders are handled by Clover, a third-party service. If you have trouble ordering online, call us and we'll take your order by phone.

Tell us about a problem

If something on this site doesn't work for you, please let us know and we'll fix it. Call (714) 699-1387 or stop by the café at 4941 Lincoln Avenue, Cypress, CA 90630.
